# Privacy Policy: Screen Recorder

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Screen Recorder is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. It records your screen, and if you choose, your camera, your microphone and the sound of your computer. This policy tells you what the extension does with that data.

## Summary

- All recording and processing happens on your device.
- The extension sends no data to the developer or to any other person or server.
- The extension has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no accounts.

## Data that the extension uses

| Data | When | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screen, window or tab video | Only after you click **Start recording** and choose what to share in the browser dialog | Kept in memory on your device until you save or close the recorder |
| Camera video | Only when you tick **Include camera** and allow the camera | Kept in memory on your device. Added to the saved video as a bubble |
| Microphone sound | Only when you tick **Include microphone** and allow the microphone | Kept in memory on your device. Added to the saved video |
| Computer sound (loopback device) | Only when you tick **Include computer sound** and allow the device | Kept in memory on your device. Added to the saved video |
| Saved video files | When you click **Save** (or when **Save at once** is on) | Saved by the browser to your Downloads folder, or to the place that you choose |
| Settings (quality, countdown, camera position and size, download subfolder, and the IDs of the camera and sound devices that you chose) | When you change a setting | Stored in the extension storage of your browser, on your device |

The browser asks for your permission before the extension can use your screen, camera or microphone. You can remove these permissions at any time in the browser settings.

## Data that the extension does not collect

The extension does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. It does not read the websites that you visit, and it has no permission to do so. It does not use remote code.

## Permissions

- **downloads:** to save your recordings as files on your device.
- **storage:** to keep your settings on your device.

## Data removal

- **Recordings:** delete the files from your Downloads folder.
- **Settings:** remove the extension. The browser then deletes its stored settings.
